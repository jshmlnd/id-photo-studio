# Surface brief — ID Photo Studio

Scope and visitor mode: Full redesign of the single-page ID Photo Studio into a dark tool. Operate: the visitor completes upload, compose, name, download. A short headline orients; the editor is the page.

Audience, job, action/task, proof/content, constraints: Personal travelers. Job: turn a portrait into a print-ready ID photo, privately. Task: upload, compose, name, download. Constraints: client-side only, 300/600 dpi output spec, no invented claims. User asked for "dark modern" built from cult-ui components; quality bar is the cult-ui showcase itself.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: the category standard (dark SaaS tool) at cult-ui finish. Memorable moment: the portrait settles into the crop stage from blur to sharp, and the export pixel count rolls to its new value whenever size or dpi changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dark tool done at showcase finish; refuses the light government-form look and the neon-glow AI-tool look.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral near-black ground (#0a0a0a), layered cult-ui texture cards with nested hairline borders, white texture primary button, one indigo accent for selection, focus and crop guide; Geist for all text, Geist Mono only for measurements; rounded 12-24px radii; soft offset shadows only.

STORY: The visitor sees it is a private photo tool, drops a portrait, drags it into the guide, picks size and format, downloads an exact print file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header with wordmark and on-device privacy note. Compact headline and one-line promise. Two-column grid: crop stage card (halo dropzone when empty) left, settings card right with segmented size, format, paper, name strip, dpi, preview and full-width download button at the rail's foot.

FORM: canon (category standard), seed:52f142cc8a9742cf

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
