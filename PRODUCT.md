# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 (existing codebase).

## Users

Primary: personal travelers — people taking passport, visa, or national ID photos for travel or government paperwork.

## Product Purpose

Turn an uploaded portrait into a print-ready ID photo quickly and privately.

## Positioning

Privacy-first and print-precise: all processing happens client-side (nothing is uploaded), and the exported photo meets print requirements (300 DPI, 1×1 or 2×2 inch output, optional white name strip).

## Operating Context

Consumer web tool; fully client-side with no backend or storage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Accepts JPEG, PNG, or WebP uploads.
- Drag-to-reposition and zoom controls.
- Export: 1×1 or 2×2 inch PNG at 300 DPI; optional white name strip with uppercase name (max 40 chars).

## Brand Commitments

Privacy-first positioning and print-precise output are the product identity.

## Evidence on Hand

None recorded.

## Product Principles

1. Privacy before convenience: nothing ever leaves the user's device.
2. Precision before shortcuts: the print spec is the floor, not an upsell.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

None recorded.
